### Hi there 👋

My name is Dietrich, but people typically call me Didi.

## Biography

Dietrich is a seasoned information systems and publishing professional who combines technical acumen with a strong business background. He joined MDPI in 2005 and has held various roles, including Assistant Editor, Managing Editor, CTO, and CEO. In May 2010, he co-founded MDPI AG, MDPI’s scholarly publishing branch, alongside Dr. Shu-Kun Lin. After a brief period away from MDPI, Dietrich returned in 2020 as a Project Manager. In this role, he played a key part in implementing web-based publishing solutions and led the frontend team in adopting a new technology stack. In April 2024, he became Head of Technical Development at MDPI, working across Product and IT to ensure the delivery of high-quality technical solutions that meet product requirements. In 2025, he was promoted once again to the role of CTO. Dietrich holds a bachelor’s degree in Business and Economics from the University of Basel and a Master of Science in Business Information Systems from FHNW. His research interests include AI-based solutions for publishing.
